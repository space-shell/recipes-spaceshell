The week's grain workhorse. Black rice cooks 1 : 1.5 with water in about
**35–40 minutes**, keeps its bite for days, and brings **~4–5g of fiber
per cooked serving** — nothing else in the rice family is close. Edamame
adds protein, the miso-sesame dressing makes it disappear, and a soft
egg or flaked fish on top turns it into dinner.

## Method

### 1. Black Rice

Rinse the rice, then cook **1 part rice : 1.5 parts water** for
**35–40 minutes**. Rest **10 minutes off the heat** before serving —
it finishes setting up.

### 2. Edamame

Cook the shelled edamame until tender, then drain.

### 3. Dressing

Whisk the white miso, rice vinegar, sesame oil, maple, and grated ginger
until smooth.

### 4. Bowl

Bowl the rice and edamame, spoon over the dressing, and finish with
scallions and sesame seeds. Top with a soft egg or flaked fish if you
want the full meal.
