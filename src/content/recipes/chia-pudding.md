Breakfast that takes five minutes and pays out about **10g of fiber per
jar**. Mix it at night, grab a jar in the morning, top with berries.
The only technique that matters is the second whisk — it's what keeps
the seeds from clumping into a solid mass.

## Method

### 1. Whisk

Whisk the chia seeds, almond milk, vanilla, and maple (if using) in a
jar or bowl until everything is evenly suspended.

### 2. Rest & Whisk Again

Rest **10 minutes**, then **whisk again**. The first rest lets the seeds
start gelling; the second whisk breaks up any clumps before they set.

### 3. Set

Jar it and refrigerate **overnight**. It thickens to a spoonable
pudding.

### 4. Finish

Top with berries. Keeps **4–5 days** — make a whole row of jars while
you're at it.
