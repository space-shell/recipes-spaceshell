One batch of [Homemade Seitan](/recipes/homemade-seitan/), three
completely different dinners. Each way below serves two, so a single
batch covers the lot — or pick a favourite and freeze half.

## Method

### 1. Stir-fry

Slice the seitan thin. Pan-fry in a little oil until the **edges
crisp**, then add sliced peppers, garlic, and ginger. Finish with a
splash of soy. Serve over something fibrous — the black rice bowl works.

### 2. BBQ

Shred or slice the seitan and toss it in BBQ sauce until thoroughly
coated. Broil for **5 minutes** until sticky and caramelised at the
edges. Watch it — the sauce goes from sticky to burnt fast.

### 3. Kebabs

Cube the seitan and marinate **1 hour or more** in olive oil, lemon,
oregano, and crushed garlic. Skewer with red onion and pepper chunks and
grill **10–12 minutes**, turning, until charred in places.
