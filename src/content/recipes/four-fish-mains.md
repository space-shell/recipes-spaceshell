Four weeknight fish mains, each serving two. They all run off one
pattern — protein plus one vegetable, cooked fast — so once the first
one clicks, the rest are autopilot. Fish doesn't batch-prep; it stays
fresh-cooked while the grains and veg handle the week ahead.

## Method

### 1. Salmon + Roasted Broccoli

Rub the salmon fillets with olive oil, salt, pepper, and lemon.
**400°F for 12–15 minutes**, with the broccoli roasting alongside on the
same tray.

### 2. Cod + Green Beans + Almonds

Cod with olive oil, lemon, and garlic — **400°F for 12–15 minutes**.
Meanwhile sauté the green beans and finish them with toasted flaked
almonds.

### 3. Shrimp + Cauliflower Rice Bowl

Shrimp, **2–3 minutes per side** in garlic butter, over sautéed
cauliflower rice. Finish with scallion and lime. From-frozen shrimp goes
straight in the pan — about 5 minutes total.

### 4. Trout + Braised Cabbage

Trout fillets pan-crisped **skin-side down** until the skin snaps.
Alongside, cabbage wedges braised with apple cider vinegar and caraway
until soft and sweet-sharp.
