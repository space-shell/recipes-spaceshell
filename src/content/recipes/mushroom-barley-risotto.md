A risotto with a spine. Hulled barley takes its time — **50–60
minutes** — but unlike pearl barley it keeps its chew through every
reheat, which makes it the rare risotto you can batch-prep. Deeply
browned mushrooms do the flavour work, and pan-crisped
[seitan strips](/recipes/homemade-seitan/) on top bring the protein.

## Method

### 1. Mushrooms

Brown the sliced mushrooms hard in oil — deeply, in batches if needed —
until they've given up their water and taken on real colour. Pale
mushrooms make a pale risotto.

### 2. Toast

Add the hulled barley and toast it in the pan for **2 minutes**, stirring
to coat.

### 3. Stock

Add the hot vegetable stock a ladle at a time, stirring, letting each
one absorb before the next, until the barley is tender with a chew —
**~50–60 minutes**. The hands-off version is fine too simmer freely in
generous stock and drain; the barley forgives you.

### 4. Finish

Beat in the parmesan and butter until glossy.

### 5. Seitan Strips

Pan-crisp the seitan strips in a little oil until browned at the edges
and pile them on top.
