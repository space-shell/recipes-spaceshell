The Sunday anchor. One tray of roasted veg covers four or five days of
sides and bowl-builders, and the four finishes below mean it never
tastes like the same meal twice. Dense, floret, and soft veg share the
oven on a stagger — start the dense stuff first.

## Method

### 1. Base Method

Oven at **425°F / 220°C**. Cut everything to a similar size, toss with
**1.5–2 tbsp olive oil per tray**, and spread in a **single layer with
space between pieces**. Salt before it goes in. Dense veg
(carrots, Brussels sprouts, cabbage wedges, red onion) needs
**25–35 min**; florets (broccoli, cauliflower) **20–25 min**; soft veg
(peppers) about **15 min** — start the dense veg first and add the soft
veg halfway through.

### 2. Lemon-Garlic + Feta

Toss the veg with olive oil, garlic, and lemon zest before roasting.
Finish with crumbled feta and herbs. This is the one that pairs with
fish mains.

### 3. Smoked Paprika + Chickpeas

Add **1 cup cooked chickpeas** to the tray, tossed in smoked paprika and
cumin — they only need **15 minutes**, so add them partway through.
Finish with lime.

### 4. Miso-Sesame

Toss the veg with **1.5 tbsp white miso + 1 tbsp sesame oil**, thinned
with a little water so it coats. Finish with sesame seeds and scallion.
Pairs with tofu or shrimp.

### 5. Curry + Yogurt

Toss with olive oil and curry powder before roasting, then finish with a
yogurt drizzle. Pairs with dal.
