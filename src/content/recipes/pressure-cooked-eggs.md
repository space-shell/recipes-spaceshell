The fix for the gray-green ring that boils into hard-cooked eggs. High
pressure for a few minutes, a short natural release, then straight into
ice water — yolks stay yellow, whites stay tender, and the batch keeps
all week for bowls, salads, and breakfasts.

## Method

### 1. Set Up

Pour **1 cup water** into the pressure cooker and set the eggs on a
trivet or in a steamer basket.

### 2. Cook

**4–5 minutes at high pressure** — 4 for jammy yolks, 5 for fully set.

### 3. Release

Let it **natural-release for 5 minutes**, then release any remaining
pressure.

### 4. Ice Bath

Straight into ice water for **5+ minutes**. This stops the cooking
dead — it's the step that keeps the yolks yellow.

### 5. Store

Keep them **unpeeled** in the fridge for up to 1 week, peeling as you
eat.
