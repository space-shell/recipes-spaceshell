The lunch that justifies the batch-cook Sunday. Puy lentils hold their
shape and their bite where other lentils collapse, the warm vinaigrette
soaks in while they're still hot, and grilled halloumi on top makes it
feel like a proper plate instead of meal prep.

## Method

### 1. Lentils

Simmer the Puy lentils for **20–25 minutes** until tender but still
holding their shape. Drain.

### 2. Vinaigrette

Whisk the olive oil, lemon juice, and Dijon, then stir through the
finely diced shallot.

### 3. Halloumi

Grill the halloumi slices **1–2 minutes per side** until golden and
blistery.

### 4. Assemble

Toss the **warm** lentils with the vinaigrette, pile into bowls, and
top with the halloumi and soft herbs. Keeps **4 days** in the fridge.
