Flour washing sounds like a faff and isn't. Knead a dough, rinse away
the starch, and what's left is pure gluten — **~25g protein per 100g**
once cooked. This is the week's anchor protein: one session gives you
~250–300g of seitan, enough for four portions across stir-fries, BBQ,
and kebabs.

## Method

### 1. Mix & Knead

Combine the flour and water and knead **8–10 minutes** into a stiff
dough. It will feel shaggy at first — keep going, it comes together.

### 2. Rest

Cover and rest **1 hour**. Or, for the best gluten development, rest it
**overnight fully submerged in cold water**.

### 3. Wash

Put the dough in a large bowl of cold water and knead it
underwater — the starch clouds the water. Pour off the cloudy water,
refill, and repeat **4–6 times** until the water runs nearly clear,
about **10–15 minutes**. What remains is the gluten ball, roughly
**250–300g**.

### 4. Rest Again

Let the gluten ball sit for **20 minutes**. This relaxes it so it
doesn't tear when you shape it.

### 5. Simmer

Shape into a log and lower it into the broth. Keep it at a **bare
simmer — never a boil** — for **45–60 minutes**, turning occasionally.
Boiling is what makes seitan spongy, so keep the heat honest.

### 6. Cool & Store

Cool the seitan **in the broth**, then store it refrigerated in that
broth for up to **1 week**. Slice, shred, or cube as needed — see
[Seitan Three Ways](/recipes/seitan-three-ways/) for what to do with it.
