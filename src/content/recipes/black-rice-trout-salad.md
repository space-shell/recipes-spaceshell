The other half of the Sunday black-rice batch. Cold rice, flaked trout,
crunchy cucumber and radish, a sharp lemon dressing — it eats like a
proper salad rather than leftover rice, and it holds **3 days** without
the trout sulking. This is the bulk-prep lunch that makes the rest of
the week easy.

## Method

### 1. Rice

Cook the black rice — **1 part rice : 1.5 parts water, 35–40
minutes** — and spread it out to cool completely before building the
salad. Warm rice wilts everything else.

### 2. Trout

Flake the cooked trout into generous pieces, keeping some structure.

### 3. Assemble

Fold together the cooled rice, trout, diced cucumber, sliced radish, and
soft herbs.

### 4. Dress

Dress with olive oil and lemon, season, and box it up. Keeps **3 days**
in the fridge.
