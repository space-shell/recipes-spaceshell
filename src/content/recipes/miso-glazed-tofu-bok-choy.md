The fastest high-protein dinner on file. Press the tofu properly and it
takes a golden crust and a sticky miso glaze like it was born for it,
and the bok choy picks up all the browned glaze left in the pan. One
skillet, start to finish in about half an hour.

## Method

### 1. Press

Press the firm tofu for **20 minutes** — a plate and a weight is fine —
then cut into cubes.

### 2. Glaze

Stir together the white miso, mirin, soy sauce, and maple syrup. It
should be thick and spoonable.

### 3. Sear

Pan-sear the tofu in a little oil until **golden on all sides**. Add the
glaze for the **last 2 minutes** only, tossing so every cube gets
coated and the glaze reduces to a shine.

### 4. Bok Choy

Sear the bok choy halves **cut-side down** in the same pan, picking up
the leftover glaze, until the cut faces are browned and the leaves are
just wilted.
